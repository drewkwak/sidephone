# Changelog

## 1.0.1

First public release.

- Mobile-size preview of the active tab in the side panel, following navigation and reloads.
- Device list matching Chrome DevTools, with device frames, browser bars and camera cutouts.
- On-screen keyboard, scroll sync, pin, rotate, pop-out window and real-size mode.
- Access to localhost at install; every other site is granted individually by the user.
